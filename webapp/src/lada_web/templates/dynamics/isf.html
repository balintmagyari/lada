Upload a NumPy .npz trajectory and get the coherent intermediate scattering function F(q,t) / F(q,0) at one scattering vector magnitude, averaged over directions drawn on a sphere of radius q. Every atom in the file takes part; the chain topology is not needed.

A magnitude q probes a length scale d = 2π / q. Statistical noise grows with lag time; ignore roughly the last 10–20 % of points when fitting relaxation times.
