Upload a stress autocorrelation file (from fix ave/correlate) and get the dynamic moduli G'(ω) and G''(ω). LaDa first computes the stress relaxation modulus G(t) in LJ reduced units (k_B = 1), then fits a Prony series to it and transforms the fit analytically into the frequency domain. G_GK is the Green-Kubo average over the 3 shear components; G_FSR uses all 5 independent components of the traceless stress tensor (3 shear plus 3 normal stress differences).
